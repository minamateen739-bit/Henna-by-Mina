About Us

I am a passionate and creative person who loves learning and growing every day. I have a strong interest in art, education, and technology. I enjoy working as a mehndi artist and love creating beautiful, meaningful designs that make people feel special.


Along with my creative work, I am studying Information Technology and also enjoy teaching science and arts. I believe education and skills can change lives, and I always try to improve myself with knowledge and hard work.


I am dedicated, honest, and motivated to build a better future while doing what I love.
